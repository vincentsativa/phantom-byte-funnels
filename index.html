// failure mode analysis

Production agents break in predictable ways.

Most agent failures are not mysterious. They are a handful of recurring modes that quietly drain your budget, your time, and your users' trust. Here is the gap PhantomByte closes for you.

🧠
reasoning loops

Stuck in a loop, burning tokens

Your agent spins in circles, calling the same tool over and over while your API bill climbs. It never finishes the job and you never find out why.

📊
context degradation

Memory that quietly forgets

By turn 20 your agent has lost the plot. It forgets earlier instructions, drifts off task, and gives answers that look right but are wrong.

🔍
tool call failures

Tools that fail silently

A tool throws or returns garbage and your agent runs with it. No error, no retry, no alert. Just bad output shipped to your users.

💰
silent money burn

You pay for failures, not results

Every broken run still costs you. The bill grows, the value does not, and nobody can tell you where the money is actually going.

Every day this continues, you ship broken agents and burn budget. PhantomByte teaches you to fix it yourself.
